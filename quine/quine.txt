A CARDIAC quine: it punches the deck that loads it.

cell  contents   notes
 03     003      the address to punch next; it starts as its own address, 03
 04     519      OUT 19   punch 002, the first bootstrap card      <- starts here
 05     520      OUT 20   punch 800, the second bootstrap card
 06     503      OUT 03   punch the address card
 07     103      CLA 03   take the address
 08     221      ADD 21   add 500, making "OUT address"
 09     610      STO 10   put that instruction in cell 10
 10     510      OUT 10   (rewritten every time: OUT address) punch the data card
 11     103      CLA 03
 12     222      ADD 22   next address
 13     603      STO 03
 14     724      SUB 24   past the last cell yet?
 15     306      TAC 06   no: punch the next pair
 16     519      OUT 19   punch 002 ...
 17     523      OUT 23   ... and 804: the pair that puts JMP 04 in cell 02
 18     900      HRS 00   halt
 19     002      data: 002
 20     800      data: 800
 21     500      data: 500, the OUT instruction with address 00
 22     001      data: 1
 23     804      data: 804, JMP 04
 24     025      data: 25, one past the last cell
