A CARDIAC quine: it punches the deck that loads it.

cell  contents   notes
 03     003      the address to punch next; it starts as its own address, 03
 04     518      OUT 18   punch 002, the loader's first card      <- starts here
 05     519      OUT 19   punch 800, the loader's second card
 06     503      OUT 03   punch the address card
 07     103      CLA 03   take the address
 08     220      ADD 20   add 500, making "OUT address"
 09     610      STO 10   put that instruction in cell 10
 10     510      OUT 10   (rewritten every time: OUT address) punch the value card
 11     103      CLA 03
 12     221      ADD 21   next address
 13     603      STO 03
 14     723      SUB 23   past the last cell yet?
 15     306      TAC 06   no: punch the next pair
 16     522      OUT 22   punch 804, the card that jumps to the start
 17     900      HRS 00   halt
 18     002      data: the loader's first card
 19     800      data: the loader's second card
 20     500      data: 500, the OUT instruction with address 00
 21     001      data: 1
 22     804      data: the last card, JMP 04
 23     024      data: 24, one past the last cell

Two cells change while it runs, and each starts out holding what it holds at the moment it is punched: cell 03
holds the address being punched, so when it punches cell 03 it holds 03; cell 10 is rewritten to OUT address
before it runs, so when the address is 10 it holds 510. There is no JMP, because JMP writes cell 99.
